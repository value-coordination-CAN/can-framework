Funded by everyone who contributes, not only by those with cash

A project can be funded across all three layers. Capital, land use, equipment, expertise, labour, pre-committed use and community contribution can each earn participation, access, or both. Suppliers can take part of their margin as a verified stake instead of cash alone, and any verified stake can be pledged for liquidity without being sold.
